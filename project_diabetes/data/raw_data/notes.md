Questions for drawing insights about diabetes.
**Demographic Patterns:**
1. Which age groups have the highest diabetes prevalence?
2. Does income level correlate with diabetes risk?
3. Are there gender differences in diabetes rates across different age groups?

**Health Status Impact:**
4. How much does having both high blood pressure AND high cholesterol increase diabetes risk compared to having just one?
5. Is there a correlation between self-reported general health and actual diabetes diagnosis?

**Lifestyle Analysis:**
6. Do physically active smokers have lower diabetes rates than inactive non-smokers?
7. What's the combined effect of poor diet (low fruits/veggies) and heavy alcohol consumption on diabetes?
8. Does physical activity compensate for poor dietary habits in preventing diabetes?

**Medical Access Barriers:**
9. Are people without healthcare access more likely to have undiagnosed or poorly managed diabetes?
10. Does cost-related lack of doctor visits correlate with worse health outcomes (higher BMI, worse GenHlth)?

**Physical Conditions:**
11. At what BMI threshold does diabetes risk significantly increase?
12. Is there a relationship between mental health and diabetes management/risk?
13. Do people with difficulty walking have higher diabetes rates even when controlling for BMI?

**Multi-Category Insights:**
14. What's the profile of the "highest risk" individual? (combining demographics, lifestyle, and health status)
15. Which single factor (or combination of 2-3 factors) is the strongest predictor of diabetes?
